Breakfast

A rich and colorful start to your day. Enjoy tables laden with freshly chopped salads, savory spreads, warm pastries, and sweet herbal tea. Everything is served in abundance — simple and delicious, exactly as breakfast should be.

This is your moment to start the day calmly, without looking at the clock. Just sit back facing the view, dip fresh pita bread, and savor the special silence of the morning hours at the Inn. It’s the best way to recharge (and fill up) before heading out to explore the area or settling in for a lazy day at the Inn.

Bedouin dinner

The Madfouna Feast

Experience the ancient Bedouin tradition of "buried" cooking. Chicken and root vegetables are slow-cooked underground on hot coals for hours, absorbing the smoky aromas of the earth. The result is a succulent, melt-in-your-mouth meal served with seasoned rice and fresh salads, a true taste of the desert.

Dinner here is much more than food – it’s a whole event of togetherness. The concept is sharing: the meal is served on a huge central platter, so everyone can taste and enjoy the abundance together — perfect for families and groups of friends. We believe food brings people closer, and in the desert you feel it even more. To prepare it fresh and hot for you, the feast is served by pre-order only — just let us know, and we’ll take care of filling your table.

All year round

The Heated Pool

Even when the desert nights turn chilly, our waters remain inviting. Step into our covered, heated pool for a soothing soak. It is the perfect place to unwind, relax tired muscles after a long hike, and enjoy a moment of pure tranquility, no matter the season.

Housed in a beautifully designed structure that echoes the desert landscape, the pool is open all year round — for a quiet night swim or a winter afternoon. It sits right next to the large open-air pool (active in the warm seasons), completing the water experience at the Inn.

Self-catering

The Communal Kitchen

For those who love to cook or just want a light snack, our spacious kitchen awaits. It offers you the freedom to prepare your own meals, from a quick morning coffee to a full family dinner, just the way you like it.

Cook like at home, in the heart of the desert. The communal kitchen is the culinary heart of the Inn: all you need to bring is your cookware and food, and the kitchen is freely available to you throughout your stay.

Our residents

The Petting Farm

A heartwarming experience for all ages. Come say hello to our resident camels, donkeys, dwarf goats and rabbits. It is a wonderful opportunity for children (and adults!) to touch, feed, and connect with nature in the most direct and gentle way.

The animals are an inseparable part of the calm atmosphere we cultivate at the Inn. Meeting them offers a rare moment to pause from the daily rush and observe the slow, natural rhythm of life.

Shared spaces

Sunsets & Bonfires

As the day ends, the desert puts on a show. Watch the sky turn fiery red over the dunes, then gather around a crackling bonfire. Whether with a guitar, sweet herbal tea, or just silence, this is where the best memories are made under the stars.

Shared spaces are scattered throughout the Inn, inviting you to find your own corner: secluded benches for watching the desert sunset, shady nooks for an afternoon rest, and campfire areas that come alive at night.

In season

The Large Pool

Our main pool is the place to be when the temperatures rise (open in season). It lies under the open sky, unroofed and unheated, looking straight out over the wild, open landscape. The cool water is exactly what you need in the middle of the desert — the perfect refreshment facing the mountains.
