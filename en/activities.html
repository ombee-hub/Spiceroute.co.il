Paintball in Tsofar – Etgar BaTamar

Ready for the ultimate challenge? At "Etgar BaTamar" – experiential paintball in the Arava – you’re invited into a desert battlefield! Among giant date palms in Moshav Tsofar awaits the perfect arena to release some adrenaline.

A perfect outing for groups, families and couples – a bonding, adrenaline-filled experience in the most special location in the south.

Walking distance from the Inn!

Shepherd for a Day – Adi Etrog

A small, homey sheep and goat farm in Moshav Tsofar invites you to take part in caring for the animals and making cheese. The yard is home to goats, sheep and dogs trained to herd the flock.

Start with a demonstration and try hand-milking, make cheese from the milk you collected, and watch the trained dogs help the shepherd guide the herd. In lambing season you can pet and bottle-feed the lambs and kids. Finish with coffee and cake, homemade bread and cheese. Visits by appointment.

5-minute walk from the Inn · Bookings: Adi +972-52-3868909

Food & Wine

Moa Winery

In 1984, Yehuda and Tzvia Friedman came to Moshav Tsofar in the Arava and founded their family farm. In 2014 the family decided to fulfil another dream and open a small family winery.

The old packing house, two empty flower coolers and a basic winemaking course gave them the idea of making wine in the Arava. The grapes come from the Ruchot plateau near Mitzpe Ramon, for a unique desert wine that embodies the Incense Route and the desert.

Nanu Café

Nanu is a home of food, art and coffee. Most of the produce comes from the Arava – from local farmers, artists and seasonal crops. The coffee is local, and so are the people.

The menu is fresh, varied, seasonal and changes daily. Nanu sits in the artists’ village of Tzukim, next to the gallery.

Trails

Hatzeva Loop (cycling)

An easy ride for the whole family, beginners and children, starting from the eastern entrance gate of Moshav Hatzeva.

Head north on the Peace Road for about 2 km to the lookout (a short walk among the marl gullies is recommended) and continue on the green trail. After 3.1 km, at the junction with the blue trail, turn right to the Hatzeva reservoir and continue north along the bank of Nahal HaArava. After 1.6 km cross the stream on the paved road and ride south between the greenhouses, and finally (3.8 km) cross back to the western bank and climb back to the moshav gate.

Trails

The Springs Route & the Incense Route

A unique route for professional and amateur cyclists, with climbs and descents in and out of the streambeds and springs – one of the few places in Israel offering such an experience close to comfortable lodging.

The springs emerge along the fault line where the eastern Negev Highlands meet the Arava Valley. Among them is Ein Tamid, hidden in a palm-lined gully with remains of ancient agriculture; south of it is a lookout named after Idan Gadish, who studied the Arabian oryx and its reintroduction to the Negev; and about 2 km southeast of Ein Shahak lies Ein Duhan, whose brackish water serves many of the area’s animals.

A navigation game in the Tzukim landscape for all ages, families and groups of any size. The game takes place around the community of Tzukim and lasts about two hours. It can be played in Hebrew or English and adapted to the size of the group.
