Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 1, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This document outlines the privacy policy of Spice Route Inn Ltd. (hereinafter: "the Inn" or "Website Management") for the website www.spiceroute.co.il. The purpose of this policy is to explain what personal information we collect, how we use it, and how we safeguard it. By using the website, its services, and its contact forms, you consent to this policy.

1. Information Collection

When you use the website, we may collect two types of information:

  • Information you provide voluntarily: Personal details such as your full name, phone number, email address, and special notes or requests, provided by you via "Contact Us" forms, newsletter subscriptions, or when making a reservation.
  • Technical information collected automatically: Anonymous statistical data collected through "Cookies" and analytics systems (such as Google Analytics), including IP addresses, browser types, pages viewed, and time spent on the site. This information does not identify you personally.

2. Use of Information

The information collected will be used solely for the following purposes:

  • To contact you and respond to inquiries submitted on the website.
  • To manage reservations and coordinate your stay at the Inn.
  • To improve the browsing experience and ensure the proper functioning of the website.
  • To send updates, promotions, and marketing content (newsletter) – only if you have explicitly given your consent. You may unsubscribe at any time.

We commit not to share your personal details with third parties without your consent, except where required by law or to complete your reservation (e.g., credit card clearing companies or an external booking engine).

3. Cookies

The website uses "Cookies" (small text files stored in your browser) for its ongoing operation, the collection of statistical data, and information security. By browsing the site, you consent to their use.

4. External Links and Systems

The website may contain links to external websites or booking systems operated by third parties (such as our room reservation engine). We are not responsible for the privacy policies or the content of these external sites. When navigating to external platforms, their respective privacy policies apply to you.

5. Data Security

We take standard precautions and utilize advanced security technologies to protect your information. However, please note that we cannot guarantee absolute immunity against unauthorized computer intrusions or illegal exposure of information over the internet.

6. Right to Review Information

Under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, every individual is entitled to review the personal information held about them in a database. If you find that the information is incorrect, incomplete, or outdated, you have the right to contact us to request its correction or deletion. Inquiries regarding this matter can be sent to: inndesert@gmail.com.

7. Changes to the Privacy Policy

Website management reserves the right to update this privacy policy from time to time. The binding version is the one published on the website at the time of use.
