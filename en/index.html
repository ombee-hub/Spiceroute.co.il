Welcome to the Inn

Where Time Stands Still

Between the sand dunes and the breathtaking sunrises, we have created a space to simply breathe.

Spice Route Inn (Khan Derech HaBesamim) offers a unique blend of authentic desert hospitality and refined modern comfort. Whether you are a couple seeking silence in a private cabin, or a group of friends gathering around the bonfire, the desert sets the stage, and you are at the heart of the experience.

Desert Oasis

The Spice Route Inn

We have created a world designed to reconnect you with nature and tranquility. Take a refreshing dip in our pools overlooking the endless desert landscape, meet the animals in our friendly farm corner, or enjoy traditional meals served on colorful mats and cushions—echoing the feasts of ancient nomads along the Spice Route.

IncludedBreakfastA rich and colorful start to your day: freshly chopped salads, savory spreads, warm pastries, and sweet herbal tea. Just sit back and savor the morning silence. Bedouin dinnerThe Madfouna FeastChicken and root vegetables slow-cooked underground on hot coals for hours, served with seasoned rice and fresh salads — a true taste of the desert (pre-order). All year roundThe Heated PoolEven when the desert nights turn chilly, our covered, heated pool remains inviting — the perfect place to unwind after a long hike. Self-cateringThe Communal KitchenOur spacious kitchen gives you the freedom to prepare your own meals, from a quick morning coffee to a full family dinner. Our residentsThe Petting FarmCome say hello to our resident camels, donkeys, goats and farm animals — touch, feed, and connect with nature. Shared spacesSunsets & BonfiresWatch the sky turn fiery red over the dunes, then gather around a crackling bonfire — this is where the best memories are made. In seasonThe Large PoolOur main open-air pool (in season) faces the wild open landscape — a cool dip in the middle of the desert.
